If the hour's arrivals all stood in the precinct at once the density would be 38.8/m² — Fruin band F. They physically cannot, and that is the point: the excess does not disappear, it backs up along the approach routes, which is where crowd collapse happens.

Toilets and drinking water

  • Toilets, at what is mapped today: the queue never clears. 37,128 people an hour against capacity for 120; the backlog grows by 37,008 every hour.
  • Toilets, at the CPHEEO norm: about 0 minutes of queueing at 4,923 units.
  • Water points, at what is mapped today: the queue never clears. 59,405 people an hour against capacity for 180; the backlog grows by 59,225 every hour.

Queues are computed per block of 20 units, not across the whole site: people queue at the block in front of them. Pooling every unit into one queue would make the wait collapse to almost nothing, which is not what anyone has experienced at a mela. OpenStreetMap is not an inventory — a facility that exists but is unmapped counts as missing here, so the gap is an upper bound until the Authority supplies its own asset list.

The rubric

  • FLAGCan the site process the hour's arrivals?35 ptsRaised when peak-hour arrivals exceed what the open ground can turn over in an hour.
  • FLAGCan it be cleared quickly in an emergency?20 ptsRaised when clearing a full site through its approach routes would take more than 60 minutes.
  • FLAGIs any approach route dangerously narrow?15 ptsRaised when the narrowest link on an approach route is under 6 m wide. This is the mechanism that killed 39 people at Ramkund in 2003.
  • FLAGAre there enough toilets?15 ptsRaised when more than 20% of the seats required by CPHEEO's community toilet norm are missing from the map.
  • FLAGAre there enough drinking water points?15 ptsRaised when more than 20% of the taps required by CPHEEO (one per 50 people) are missing from the map.

The score is the combined weight of the checks that failed. It ranks what to resolve first. It is not a prediction, not a safety certificate, and not a statement about any authority's preparations.
